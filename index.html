GEOSPATIAL · RISK ANALYSIS 26 wards · 3 metrics

Flood Risk Exposure by Ward — Newcastle

Overlaid Environment Agency Flood Zone 2/3 data with ward boundaries and Census 2021 population to compute three complementary risk metrics per ward: proportional exposure, absolute flood zone area, and risk relative to population. Identified Parklands as the highest-risk ward across all three measures, and surfaced how absolute and proportional risk can diverge sharply between wards of different sizes.

About

I started in geology — a First Class Honours degree in Geology & Mineral Science, plus a GIS internship at StrongFoot Geophysical Services — before moving into data analytics. Rather than leaving that background behind, I've built on it: geospatial data science lets me apply the spatial reasoning from geology to the kind of location-based questions that turn up in public health, infrastructure, and environmental data.

CompTIA Data+ certified, and currently deepening that specialization through applied projects — from ONS ward-level health accessibility mapping to earlier ArcGIS Pro coursework with UC Davis.
